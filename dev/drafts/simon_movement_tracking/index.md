# Simon task with movement tracking

Source: Wirth, Foerster, Kunde & Pfister (2020), *Behavior Research Methods*, Experiment 1. doi:10.3758/s13428-020-01409-0

## Research question

**What, and why.** A question about a method: tracking mouse or finger
movements is used to observe how a decision unfolds. Do design choices that
look trivial, such as the input device, change the results?

**How.** A Simon task performed as a movement to a left or right target area;
the side on which the signalling colour appears is irrelevant and matches the
movement (compatible) or not. Three setups are compared on whether they show
the compatibility effect and its reduction after an incompatible trial.

**Part used.** That reduction in one of the mouse setups.
