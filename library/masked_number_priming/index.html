    Masked number priming

    Source: Zerweck et al. (2021), Attention, Perception, & Psychophysics, Experiment 3. doi:10.3758/s13414-021-02312-2

    Takeaway. A complete crossing of two stimuli from the same set contains the pairs with the same item twice, and those can only be congruent. When you remove them, check what the first stimulus then says about the second: the second must be equally expected in every compared condition.

    Research question

    What, and why. Unconscious processing: is the magnitude of a number processed when the number is masked and cannot be reported? The paper tests the standard evidence for this by comparing a direct and an indirect measure of the masked number.

    How. Masked priming: a masked number (the prime) precedes a visible one (the target), which is classified as smaller or larger than five. If the prime's magnitude was processed, responses are faster when both fall on the same side of five (the priming effect).

    Part used. The priming effect in the indirect task.

    Analysis

    Response time on congruent against incongruent trials, within participants.

    Design as published

    Decision Chosen Reason given What follows
    Numbers 1, 4, 6, 9, as digit or word Replicates Dehaene et al. (1998) Two numbers on each side of five
    Primes and targets Same four numbers Same A prime can equal its target
    Pairs All 16 number pairs x 4 notation combinations, once per block (64 trials) None Half congruent; a quarter show the same number twice
    Order Random per block None The previous trial is not controlled

    The design is counterbalanced, not only randomised: prime and target are completely crossed. The same-number trials are cells of that crossing, so they are a consequence of the counterbalancing and not an accident of sampling.

    What to balance

    Assumption. Congruent and incongruent trials differ only in whether prime and target share the category.

    Where it fails. Half of the congruent trials repeat the number; no incongruent trial can.

    different number same number
    congruent 16 16
    incongruent 32 0

    A congruent response may be faster because the number repeats, not because the category matches. Reordering trials cannot change this: an incongruent pair with the same number does not exist.

    Options

    In the design (functions in design.py):

    Option Same-number pairs Congruent share Trials per congruent : incongruent pair Cost
    proposed: each prime has one congruent and one fixed incongruent target none 1/2 8 : 8 Half of the incongruent pairs are not shown to a participant; use two mappings across participants. Two targets per prime, each expected at 50%.
    drop_identical_double_congruent none 1/2 8 : 4 After a prime, its congruent target is twice as likely as each incongruent target (50% against 25%), so congruent targets are the expected numbers. Congruency itself and the response stay at one half.
    drop_identical_only none 1/3 4 : 4 Incongruent trials are twice as frequent as congruent ones; 48 trials.
    separate_number_sets: primes 1, 4, 6, 9, targets 2, 3, 7, 8 none 1/2 4 : 4 Primes never occur as targets, which is a different question (priming by numbers never responded to).

    In the analysis, with the published design:

    Option Cost
    Leave the same-number trials out of the congruent mean Congruent mean from 16 trials per block, incongruent from 32. Participants still saw the repetitions.
    Three trial types: same number, congruent with different numbers, incongruent Same trial counts. Identity cannot be crossed with congruency, because one of the four cells does not exist.

    Unequal in every option. Numerical distance: congruent pairs of different numbers are 3 apart, incongruent pairs 2, 5 or 8 (5 in proposed).

    None of the options is the correct one; which to take depends on the claim.

    Design to propose

    proposed(). Raise number identity with the user, show this design, and name the alternatives with their costs.

    Prime and target stay separate factors, crossed as prime x congruency. Two constraints remove what must not occur: Exclude the trials with the same number, and Exclude the incongruent pairs outside the mapping.

    When checking any option, state what the prime predicts separately for the trial type, the response and the target number. In drop_identical_double_congruent the first two stay at one half and only the third is unequal.
